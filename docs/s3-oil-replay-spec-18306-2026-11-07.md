## §3. What production actually does

| Stage | Source-backed behaviour to reproduce |
|---|---|
| Session | Energy uses weekdays 07:00–20:00 UTC: 15:00–04:00 next day SGT. Eligible five-second entry windows start at 07:30, 08:00, …, 19:00 UTC: **24 marks**. No entry at 19:30. These are the program's registered hours, not a new assertion about exchange opening hours. |
| Preparation | `strategy3::preopen` starts T−20 minutes, aims to finish by T−10, and records its cutoff/completion. `load_day` normally requests 45 calendar days of M30 history; an accepted prepared table is reused. The actual session-open price is established separately. |
| Bands | `band::sigma_table` takes the latest 14 usable prior-session absolute open-to-mark returns at each elapsed half-hour, searching at most 30 prior sessions. Missing observations remain missing. Today's future closes cannot supply today's bands. |
| Open and side | The session-open M30 bar must have positive tick volume. At the first eligible quote for each mark, the shared midpoint/spread rule selects outside-band long/short or no signal. The mark is durably consumed, including an inside-band decision. |
| R and C | R is the band half-width rounded to the broker price grid. C uses the prepared median spread, round-trip commission in price units and 24 bp per side. The stored spread estimator takes up to 50,000 two-sided rows in the preceding seven days. Commission uses the measured class book, otherwise its explicitly estimated overall median, and the quote-to-deposit rate. |
| Cost veto | C >= R creates a durable account/symbol/session veto. A later mark does not reopen it. The registered direct-predecessor veto survives the upgrade; unrelated hashes do not. |
| Admission | Owner pause, current account equity, live account-wide admission, symbol/hash Kelly history, quantity rounding, open-risk budget and final broker gate remain applicable. Fewer than 20 eligible Kelly closes are not an estimated edge. Positive Kelly does not increase size. |
| Dispatch | Before the market frame is written: original five-second deadline, connection identity, unchanged account revision, fresh non-crossed bid/ask (at most six seconds), same original side and **C recomputed at the current executable price**. A cost refusal vetoes the session; other refusals do not. The frozen TP/SL payload is not refitted. |
| Position and exits | Any actual/partial fill must be reconciled. Opening R and the recorded target allocation survive later stop amendments. The full exit path includes broker SL/TP, client TP1 where quantity permits, original-R trailing, session-end close and broker acknowledgements/rejections. No automatic reversal is added. |

## §4. Reuse and material differences

The existing [native S3 replay](../crates/jiaoyi-core/src/research/s3_ticks.rs)
already supports a session-length request up to 24 hours, shared bands and
targets, durable-in-run cost veto, one simulated entry, original-R trailing
every 30 seconds, no new trailing proposal inside the final half-hour, broker
bracket priority and a five-second full-session closing tail. A shorter request
censors a remaining position; it must not invent a forced exit.

| Difference | Evidence / required treatment |
|---|---|
| Oil routing | `s3_signal::Request::validate` currently admits BTC/NatGas for current S3. Major FX is diagnostic-only; original-20 names work through separate candidate modes. Merely changing the symbol in a current-S3 request will fail. Add only the two explicit oil names to the intended research path. |
| History contract | Research requests 50 days and its `Bar` stores only time/open/close. Production preparation uses 45 days and checks positive opening-bar tick volume. Retain the raw history but form the exact production view; add explicit volume/provenance for the new format. Missing volume is unknown, never assumed positive. Preserve old fixtures as legacy scenarios. |
| Preparation causality | Historical availability timestamps and preopen receipts are distinct from bar timestamps. Replay from a recorded prepared table where available; otherwise label deterministic recomputation and its cutoff. Never substitute today's SQL `now()` spread median or current commission book into an earlier decision. |
| Final dispatch | Replay rechecks direction at the next fresh quote but does not recheck C there. It also lacks actual writer delay, connection/account revisions, rejection or partial-transmission state. Add the deterministic final cost/refusal transition; keep unknown transport state explicit. Do not treat a simulated fill quote as a measured dispatch event. |
| Volume and account state | Replay accepts fixed native volume and only current digits/minimum/step. That is not historical risk sizing, Kelly, margin or portfolio admission. A fixed-volume run must say `account_admission = not_reconstructed`; account-level claims require contemporaneous snapshots. Never infer that 100 native units equals a particular number of lots. |
| Quote clocks and quality | Separate historical sides lack cross-side event order. The native assembler groups equal-millisecond events, marks stale/crossed/missing sides and gaps, and cannot prove continuous broker truth. Production uses receipt clocks for guards. Preserve both clocks and every native quality flag; do not erase flags with a fresh-quote-only Python adapter. |
| Exits and broker truth | The engine assumes next-quote TP1/amend acknowledgement and first quote at/after session end. It currently receives stale-marked snapshots as well as fresh ones. Client-action freshness, actual acknowledgements, proposed versus acknowledged stops, pending proposals at the final-half-hour boundary and absent closing quotes need explicit parity cases. Tick gaps alone do not prove broker packet loss. |

Keep [the existing exit engine](../crates/jiaoyi-core/src/research/exit_replay.rs).
Do not silently change its common behaviour to make one oil sample profitable.
Any behaviour correction needs a minimal counterexample and unchanged legacy
scenario expectations, or an explicit versioned correction with an explained
old/new result. A code review identifies these gaps; it does not quantify their
effect on win rate.

## §6. Minimum evidence contract for the eventual replay

Retain one bounded immutable source artifact and one small manifest per
account/symbol/session, outside Git for raw ticks. Do not create a new telemetry
database, duplicate historical corpus or permanent collector in this step.

1. **Identity:** source commit/file hashes, v7 hash, request/hash, environment,
   account, exact name-to-symbol mapping and its effective time, UTC session,
   extraction time, source and dataset classification. Keep broker data time,
   local receipt time and acquisition time separate.
2. **Preparation:** the applicable 45-day M30 view, positive-volume opening bar,
   exact prior observations per mark, cutoff and prepared-table receipt or an
   explicit recomputation label. Missing observations must produce a reason,
   not be repaired with future data or an extra history search.
3. **Quotes:** both sides through the full 13-hour session plus the five-second
   closing tail, bounds/pages/counts and native ambiguity/gap flags. A quote
   before the opening boundary may initialise a side only with its true age
   and provenance. No unbounded fill-forward.
4. **Costs and contract:** contemporaneous prepared spread, sample count/as-of
   time, commission source, conversion rate, price/quantity grids and lot-size
   mapping. Use measured paired entry/exit benchmarks only when actually
   linked; entry slippage is not a round trip. Missing history stays unknown.
5. **Optional account fidelity:** settings version, pauses, equity, existing
   positions/pending orders, margin evidence, owned hash-scoped closes, account
   revision and connection state at the decision. Without these, publish only
   a fixed-volume strategy scenario and an explicit admission limitation.

Executable buy/ask and sell/bid fills already incorporate spread. Deduct the
explicit commission/adverse-slippage scenario once, with volume-weighted exits
and original-R denominator. The reserve C determines admission/target geometry;
it is not a second spread cash charge. Relevant financing or conversion costs
cannot silently become zero. Qualified net results remain null when required
cost or path evidence is missing; exploratory scenario results may be shown
separately with their assumptions.
