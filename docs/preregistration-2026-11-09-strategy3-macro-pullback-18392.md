## Sessions and causal preparation

Crypto uses six four-hour windows anchored at 00:00, 04:00, 08:00, 12:00, 16:00 and 20:00 UTC. Traditional core instruments evaluate across the broker-active Asian, London and New York parts of the trading day. London reference is 07:00 UTC (15:00 SGT); New York reference is 09:30 America/New_York. Asian reopening and the daily close follow the symbol's declared broker schedule, including the different metals and Brent openings. Closed-market periods, broker maintenance, holidays, missing quotes and incomplete history never imply permission to trade.

Each window has an identity based on its exact UTC opening timestamp. Preparation, vetoes, arm restoration, entry accounting and attempt keys must distinguish multiple windows on the same date. There is at most one recorded entry per symbol/window. Legacy date-scoped records are interpreted under their own registration.

Preparation uses the corresponding historical window over the bounded 45-calendar-day M30 history. Five- and 20-day context refers to prior days at the corresponding window, not merely five or twenty successive four-hour blocks. Every consumed source bar must precede the current opening reference. No missing bar or opening midpoint is fabricated. Session open is evidenced by fresh two-sided broker quotes; a one-sided M30 bid open is not a substitute. Broker-open offsets are explicit, so an 18:01 reopening cannot be falsely assessed against an 18:00 executable quote.

The first half-hour uses the prior-history first-half-hour sigma as a conservative fixed threshold, alongside the 48 bp floor. Subsequent sigma uses the prepared historical marks. Production may assess a fresh qualifying impulse throughout a half-hour slot; a nonqualifying opening quote does not consume the slot. Historical half-hour decision functions remain available for old registrations and replay. A structurally valid crypto arm expires four hours after its impulse and may continue through the adjacent window boundary; resetting a window alone must not cancel it at 07:30 SGT. A new position records the close of the active execution window as its exit deadline, separately from its original arm window, so a carried setup cannot inherit an already-past close. Existing positions retain their recorded deadlines. Unarmed impulse discovery is assessed at most once per second; an armed setup checks every fresh quote. Missing readiness can still refuse an entry; no zero-latency data availability is claimed.

## Prepared admission and execution

Prepare the plan, symbol and cost inputs, account/risk state and margin evidence before a touch, using an armed/background path. A prepared admission is bound to its exact command and policy, broker generation and account revision, local admission epoch, source freshness and finite expiry. Broker events, another local entry/uncertain send, stale evidence or changed settings invalidate reuse. Account-wide entry locks and the live one-program-position rule remain. Failure to prepare is an explicit refusal, not permission to fall back to an unverified order.

At the first fresh 50% retracement touch, retain its quote and timestamp. The final writer rechecks current quotes, deadline, side, origin, cost, peer and account evidence. The owner permits up to 0.10 of the original impulse range of bounce beyond the frozen retracement level, only after a genuine touch. Conservative sizing and relative brackets fund the admitted writer-quote range; a quote beyond that range refuses the frame. Plain market-order transport does not guarantee the eventual fill lies within that range: post-dispatch slippage can shift an absolute stop relative to the impulse origin, while the fixed relative bracket distances remain unchanged. Initial touch is not relaxed into a pre-touch breakout entry. The broker's eventual fill and slippage remain separately measured.

Exactly-once intent durability is critical and remains before the broker write. Noncritical diagnostics may be moved off the critical path; missing intent durability must still refuse a send. The sub-250 ms pre-dispatch duration is a target to measure with tests and production receipts, not a guarantee derived from compilation or a mocked benchmark. An uncertain send is never retried as though nothing happened.

Required conversion pairs are subscribed and warmed; cached bid/ask evidence must be fresh, ordered and directionally conservative for loss reserves. Missing or stale currency evidence remains a refusal. A historical bar is not silently promoted into a fresh executable conversion rate.

Its actual cash risk must remain within the existing `trading.min_volume_max_risk_pct`, aggregate `trading.s3_open_risk_pct` and margin floor. The normal target risk percentage is not rewritten. Live continues to use one broker-minimum program trade at a time under its existing risk ceiling. Neither a minimum-size floor nor a favourable margin ratio grants unlimited risk.
