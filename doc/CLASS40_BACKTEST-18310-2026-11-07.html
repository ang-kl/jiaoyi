These are existing research conventions, not proven best sessions.

Frozen rules: high-efficiency first-hour momentum and reversal, low-efficiency reversal, and each rule's existing observed-feature counterpart. They use a causal first-hour range and next strictly later fresh quote. All six are shown; the report selects no winner. They are class research hypotheses, not a replay of production S3's entry preparation or the account admission state.

Cost scenario: 24 basis points per side, zero explicit round-trip commission, observed bid/ask spread charged once. These assumptions are not broker-calibrated. A cost reserve at least as large as original R vetoes the entry. The engine uses SL, TP planning, conditional partial TP, fixed-stop comparison, 30-second original-R trailing proposals and session close. Quantity is the current broker minimum, not historical account risk sizing. At the minimum quantity a partial exit may be unavailable; the target planner handles that constraint.

§3. Evidence and interpretation

Every symbol has two predeclared jobs. Collection failures, empty history, missing features, cost vetoes, censored exits and losses remain distinct. Only fully closed scenario positions enter win rates; zero closes yields N/A. PF is gross positive scenario R divided by absolute negative scenario R, not a dollar portfolio PF. Do not pool competing hypotheses or fixed/trailing arms as independent trades.

Historical tick completeness, same-millisecond cross-side sequencing, historical contract terms, actual execution latency, commission/funding and account admission are unverified. Long gaps are flags, not automatically proof of dropped broker data. Current contracts were rechecked against all 40 exact symbols before collection. No order, live strategy parameter, live symbol universe or validation seal changed.
